Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Prachi Nutrition website and practice portal, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Important: This is the platform privacy notice for Prachi Nutrition. Your dietitian may separately provide a professional privacy notice or consent form for clinical services and records.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to visitors, clients, dietitians and other account holders using the Prachi Nutrition website and online portal (the “Platform”). “Prachi Nutrition”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of this Platform. For privacy questions, contact hello@prachisayani.online.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number, account type and account status.
  • Professional information: dietitian credentials, registration details, practice information, profile details and verification status.
  • Care and service information: consultation requests, appointments, messages, diet plans, invoices and information you choose to enter into features such as food diaries, hydration logs or progress records.
  • Technical information: information necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the Platform, which may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Enquiries: details you provide when contacting us or submitting a website enquiry.

Please do not submit information that is not needed for your care or service request. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details in chat or free-text fields.

3. How we use information

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide the Platform’s features, connect clients with dietitians where requested or authorised, facilitate consultation workflows, display records you or your care team add, respond to enquiries, maintain service reliability, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. When information is shared

Information may be made available to the dietitian or practice you choose or are connected with, and to authorised platform administrators where needed for account support, verification, safety or platform operations. We may use service providers that host, secure or deliver parts of the Platform, including infrastructure, database and email-delivery providers. They process information to provide services to us and subject to applicable contractual or legal safeguards.

We do not describe account or health-related information as public profile information unless you intentionally submit it to a public-facing profile field. We do not sell personal information.

5. Health and nutrition information

Information about food, health, symptoms, measurements or nutrition care may be sensitive. Only provide information through the Platform when you understand why it is requested and who may access it. The Platform supports communication and practice workflows; it is not an emergency service and does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access and secure service infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Platform, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or comply with law. Retention periods may differ by record type and applicable professional or legal requirements.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where required for legitimate operational, professional or legal reasons. You can also stop using the Platform and sign out of your account. Browser-stored preferences may remain on the device until cleared.

8. Cookies and local storage

The Platform may use browser storage for session authentication and to remember preferences or drafts on your device. Your browser settings can help you manage or clear locally stored data; clearing it may affect sign-in or saved preferences.

9. Children

The Platform is not designed for independent use by children. A parent, guardian or legally authorised representative should contact the relevant professional before submitting information for a child.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Platform or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@prachisayani.online.
