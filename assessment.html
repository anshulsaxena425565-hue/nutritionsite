Anthropometry measurement

Measurements help describe body size and nutritional status. Please use a measuring tape and weighing scale where available. Open “How to measure” for step-by-step guidance.

How to measure height
Height is your standing length from floor to the top of your head.
  1. Remove shoes and bulky hair accessories.
  2. Stand upright against a flat wall or stadiometer, with heels together.
  3. Keep your head level and look straight ahead.
  4. Ask someone to mark the top of your head and measure from the floor.
Enter a height between 50 and 250 cm.
kilograms
kg
How to measure weight
Use a stable, reliable weighing scale.
  1. Place the scale on a hard, level floor.
  2. Weigh without shoes and in light clothing.
  3. Stand still in the centre of the scale.
  4. Record the value shown in kilograms.
Enter a weight between 10 and 500 kg.

Where exactly is the waist?
Waist circumference is measured around your abdomen, midway between the lowest rib and the top of your hip bone.
  1. Stand relaxed, feet together, with your abdomen uncovered if possible.
  2. Find the bottom of your lowest rib and the top of your hip bone.
  3. Place a non-stretch tape midway between those two points.
  4. Keep the tape level all the way around, snug but not pressing into the skin.
  5. Read the measurement after a normal breath out. Do not pull your stomach in.

It is not necessarily at the belly button or the narrowest part of the waist.

Enter a waist measurement between 30 and 250 cm.
centimetres
cm
What exactly is hip circumference?
Hip circumference is the distance around the widest part of your buttocks/hips.
  1. Stand upright with your feet together and weight evenly distributed.
  2. Wrap a non-stretch measuring tape around the widest/fullest part of your buttocks.
  3. Check from the side in a mirror so the tape stays horizontal and parallel to the floor.
  4. Keep the tape gently touching the body. It should not dig into or compress the skin.
  5. Read the value in centimetres.

Tip: Waist is measured higher on the abdomen. Hip circumference is measured lower, around the fullest part of the buttocks. Do not measure around the hip bones only.
