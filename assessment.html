How to measure height
HeightRemove shoes. Stand upright against a flat wall or stadiometer, heels together, looking straight ahead. Record from the floor to the top of the head. Ask someone to help for a more reliable reading.
kg
How to measure weight
WeightUse a reliable digital scale on a firm, level floor. Weigh in light clothing, ideally at a similar time of day when comparing readings. A single reading is only one point in time.
kg
Your typical weight before any recent change, if known.
cm
Where exactly is the waist?
Waist circumferenceStand relaxed. Place a non-stretch tape midway between the lowest palpable rib and the top of the hip bone (iliac crest). Keep the tape horizontal all the way around. Measure after a normal breath out; keep it snug without compressing the skin. If unsure, leave it blank for your clinician.
cm
How do I measure hip circumference?
Hip circumferenceThis is the measurement around the widest part of your buttocks. Stand with feet together. Wrap the tape around the widest point, keeping it level and parallel to the floor. It should touch the body without pressing into the skin. A mirror or helper can make it easier.
cm
What is MUAC?
Mid-upper arm circumferenceMUAC is the circumference around the upper arm at its midpoint. The midpoint is found between the bony tip of the shoulder (acromion) and the tip of the elbow (olecranon). It is best measured by a trained person using a non-stretch tape, with the arm relaxed.
cm
Where do I measure the calf?
Calf circumferenceMeasure around the widest part of the calf. Keep the tape level and snug without compressing the tissue. A seated position with the knee bent or a relaxed standing position can be used consistently. If you are unsure, leave this for the clinician.

Body mass index (BMI) · estimate—

Weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

Waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) · estimate—

Waist circumference ÷ hip circumference, using the same unit.
